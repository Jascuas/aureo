# Architecture and Design Exceptions

## Purpose

This file contains the only approved scoped deviations from `AGENTS.md`,
`docs/PRODUCT.md`, or `docs/DESIGN.md`. Existing code, historical precedent,
delivery pressure, and entries in `docs/CURRENT_REFACTORS.md` do not create an
exception.

Adding, expanding, or renewing an exception requires explicit human approval.
Prefer remediation with a Plane work item over a permanent exception.

## Required entry format

### AUR-EXC-NNN — Short title

- **Status:** Proposed | Approved | Expired | Removed
- **Authority affected:** `AGENTS.md` | `docs/PRODUCT.md` | `docs/DESIGN.md`
- **Scope:** Exact files, routes, modules, or behavior.
- **Reason:** Why compliance is currently disproportionate or impossible.
- **Decision owner:** Human approver.
- **Approved on:** YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Expires or review date:** YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Constraints:** What the exception does not permit.
- **Remediation:** Plane identifier or explicit reason no remediation is planned.
- **Verification:** How reviewers confirm the exception remains within scope.
